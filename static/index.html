Device Connections

Enter the addresses of your projector and the Raspberry Pi running PGenerator (or click Scan Network to find them automatically), then click Save & Test. Saving is what makes the calibration run use these addresses — testing alone doesn't store them.

Connect the projector's RS-232C port (D-Sub 9-pin) to your machine via a USB-to-serial adapter. FTDI-based adapters (FT232R) are recommended. On macOS the port appears as /dev/cu.usbserial-XXXXXXXX.

    Command Probe (first run only)

    The probe confirms your projector accepts the white-balance and picture-mode commands and saves them to configs/command_table.json — calibration can't adjust anything without them. First enable Control4/AMX in the projector's Network menu; without it the 5040UB rejects every settings command. Run it once; the green check sticks for future sessions.

    4
    Colorimeter Placement (CCDIS3)
    • ⚪
      Remove the white diffuser lens cap from the CCDIS3
      The CCDIS3 has a projector measurement mode — it does NOT use the suction-cup flush contact method. The lens must be uncapped to receive projected light.
    • ⚪
      Mount colorimeter on a tripod, centered on screen
      Position horizontally and vertically centered on the projected image area. Typical distance: 6–18 inches from the screen surface. Aim the sensor face directly at the screen.
    • ⚪
      Ensure no direct ambient light hits the sensor face from the side
      Side-angle ambient light creates stray light contamination that skews chromaticity readings. Shield the sensor from room lights if needed.
    • ⚪
      Dim or turn off all room lights
      Ambient light adds to the projected image luminance. For accurate black/grey measurements, the room should be as dark as possible.

    Warming Up

    Allow the projector to warm up for at least 30 minutes for stable color measurements. Calibration unlocks when Y luminance variance drops below 0.5% over 3 consecutive readings.

      First time? Tick Dry Run to watch the full measure-and-correct loop without changing anything on the projector. Every run first records all picture settings and resets Color, Tint, Skin tone and image processing to neutral. All then runs: Brightness and Contrast measured with the colorimeter (lowest black before shadows crush, most light before white clips) → Color Temp nearest D65 and two-point white balance keeping as much light as possible → all 9 custom-gamma points solved together against a 5–95% grey ramp — repeated until a pass changes nothing → automatic color correction (the app drives the RGBCMY menu with remote-control key presses; leave the remote alone) → verification of white, the colors, the grey ramp and 25/50/75% saturation sweeps. Corrections aim for ΔE 0.5; verification passes at ΔE 1.0. Turn Auto Iris off in the projector's Image menu first and keep the room dark. The projector stores SDR and HDR10 settings separately, so calibrate each mode in its own session.
